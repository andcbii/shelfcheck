# Use standalone episode coverage mappings as a missing-episode fallback

Providers may describe the same content as one combined episode or several split episodes. Instance-local manual coverage mappings will relate a complete set of TMDB episode records to a complete set of TVDB episode records, initially for Plex scans only. For this manual check, owning every episode in either set grants coverage for the other set; an incomplete set grants no additional coverage, because treating individual members as interchangeable would incorrectly let one part satisfy the entire combined episode.

Every existing matching check runs first, including direct ownership, shared IMDb identity, Trakt crosswalks, TMDB external-ID crosswalks, and enabled compound-episode matching. Only if an episode would still be missing does Shelfcheck check its manual coverage mapping against corrected, directly owned episode identities across the Plex inventory, regardless of parent-show associations. This is the final matching step before adding an episode to missing results. The manual check adds coverage while preserving existing automatic ownership decisions; the complete-set requirement governs coverage added by the mapping, not results already satisfied by normal matching.

For the Blaze Christmas special, the relationship is TMDB episode `6741845` to the complete set of TVDB episodes `11551379` and `11551380`. A directly owned complete TMDB special can cover either otherwise-missing TVDB part; both directly owned TVDB parts can cover the otherwise-missing TMDB special. Owning only one TVDB part adds no coverage for the other part or the complete special.

Each provider episode belongs to at most one coverage mapping; selecting a member of an existing mapping opens that mapping for editing. Mappings may cross season and provider show boundaries after every record resolves and its show, title, and numbering are presented for review. Restricting these links to matching coordinates or parent series would preserve the provider disagreements this feature is intended to correct.

Episode coverage mappings are standalone: they require no show mapping or primary catalogue configuration. An explicit episode link may cross otherwise unassociated shows. Deleting a related show mapping retains independently saved episode mappings and compatible ownership corrections; bulk deletion removes exactly the selected mappings without cascading to those independent records.

When evaluating manual coverage, Plex items carrying conflicting combined and split episode identities require an explicit ownership correction before granting additional coverage through the mapping. The user identifies whether an item contains the complete combined episode or selected parts; this correction is used by the manual check, stays in Shelfcheck, and does not modify Plex or normal matching. Without this distinction, a file containing only Part 1 could expose a combined episode ID and incorrectly add coverage for Part 2.

Corrected direct ownership used by the manual check aggregates across all Plex TV libraries included in a scan, including items from ignored shows. Ignore continues to suppress a show's own missing results and provider comparison; it does not prevent that show's owned content from satisfying mapped content elsewhere. Inferred coverage from either automatic crosswalks or manual mappings does not become direct ownership that can satisfy a mapping's prerequisites. An ownership correction must be invalidated when its Plex item changes or its selected content becomes incompatible with an edited mapping.

Mappings persist independently of inventory, scan reports, and caches, including when neither side is currently owned. Saving, editing, or deleting a mapping automatically rechecks affected shows; changes made during an active scan queue a subsequent recheck, with results shown as pending. Both the old and new members of an edited mapping must be rechecked so previously granted coverage can be withdrawn.

A confirmed deleted provider record leaves its mapping available for repair as **Needs review** and prevents additional coverage until repaired. Temporary provider outages retain the mapping with pending results. The manager must support bulk deletion of broken mappings as well as individual repair and deletion.

An incomplete mapping with remaining missing episodes appears as one grouped result showing directly owned members, missing members, and alternative complete sets, with each provider's own identities and numbering preserved. Members already satisfied by normal matching keep that status; their inferred coverage does not complete a source set for the manual check. Users can create a mapping from a missing episode's **Link episodes** action by supplying provider episode URLs or IDs and reviewing resolved records; a persistent **Manual Coverage** manager supports later edits and deletion even after the group disappears from missing results. Show records and episode records must be distinguished during input resolution so a whole-show identity is not mistaken for an episode identity.
